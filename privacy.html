Privacy Policy — BedsEye: iCal Availability Calendar

Last updated: October 29, 2026

BedsEye (“the extension”) is a Chrome extension that shows availability from iCal (.ics) calendar feeds you choose to connect. This policy describes what information the extension handles and how it is used.

Summary

  • We do not operate a BedsEye account system or a backend that receives your calendar data.
  • Calendar URLs, settings, and cached events are stored locally in your browser via Chrome extension storage.
  • The extension fetches iCal data only from URLs you enter, when you connect or refresh feeds.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising inside the extension.

Information you provide

You may add one or more calendar feed URLs (for example from Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, NightsBridge, Google Calendar export links, or other platforms), optional labels, and which feeds are enabled. This information is saved in chrome.storage.local on your device so the extension can show your calendars on later visits.

Information from your calendar feeds

When you connect or refresh a feed, the extension’s background service worker sends a request to the URL you provided and downloads the iCal file as text. The extension parses that text (for example reservation dates with check-in and check-out) to display blocked and open days in the popup. Parsed events may be cached locally to improve load time. Content of iCal files is determined by your booking platform, not by BedsEye.

Network access

The extension requests broad host permissions because feed URLs can be hosted on many different domains. Network requests are limited to fetching iCal data from URLs you saved. The extension does not read or change ordinary web pages you browse.

Remote code

All extension code is included in the package installed from the Chrome Web Store. The extension does not download or execute remote JavaScript. iCal downloads are data only, parsed locally.

Clipboard

If you select dates and use copy actions in the popup, availability text is copied to your clipboard only when you choose to do so.

What we do not collect

The extension does not send your calendar URLs, events, or usage to a server operated by the extension developer for analytics, marketing, or profiling. We do not require you to create an account to use the extension.

Chrome Web Store

Distribution and updates are handled through the Google Chrome Web Store. Google’s own privacy practices apply to the store and browser separately from this policy.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the extension after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy questions about BedsEye, contact the developer via the support channel listed on the extension’s Chrome Web Store listing, or email: anton@stayhere.co.za.
